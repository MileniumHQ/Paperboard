Your local-first tool for everything.

Paperboard has various apps (called panels) that simplify a variety of local tasks into simple, clean interfaces that run entirely on your computer. You don't need any terminal knowledge or programming experience; Paperboard works instantaneously and can be set up faster than you can read this paragraph.

Paperboard runs anywhere.

Paperboard is 100% compatible with macOS, Windows, and Linux on both x86 and ARM. You can run Paperboard on a VPS, an old laptop, or a Raspberry Pi and control it as if it were running locally on your primary desktop.

Easier, Better, Faster, Stronger

Paperboard is designed from scratch to be intuitive, fast, and incredibly easy to use. And it's not just words, we can prove it.
