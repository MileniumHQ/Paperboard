We invited Jet the Hawk to benchmark the cold start. He arrived on extreme gear, declined the stopwatch, and said he could beat it on foot. He could not beat it on foot. He then beat it on extreme gear, which we are not counting because extreme gear is not a supported platform.

## The benchmark

Jet recorded a time, refused to write it down, and left. We reconstructed the number from a wind tunnel and a rumor. The reconstructed number is faster than the spin dash and slower than the hawk. This is the most precise result we have ever published.

## Follow-up

Next quarter we will benchmark warm starts, provided nobody brings a board. The wind tunnel has been booked for four seconds.
