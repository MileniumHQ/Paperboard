Every release, someone on the team claims the app starts faster than a spin dash. Every release, nobody produces the stopwatch. This release, we produced the stopwatch. It was gold, it was shaped like a ring, and it did not belong to us.

## The measurement

We launched the app forty times on a laptop that has seen things. The median was a number we wrote on a napkin. The mean was a different number, also on the napkin. A hedgehog reviewed the napkin and declined to comment, which we are counting as peer review.

## The result

Paperboard starts quickly enough that the loading screen has time to display one (1) chili dog before the panel library appears. We consider the loading screen retired. It now lives on a farm where it chases smaller loading screens.

Next release we plan to measure shutdown. The stopwatch is still gold. The napkin has been laminated for posterity.
