This post is a full audit of every snack inside the panel library. There is one chili dog. There have always been one chili dog. When we add a panel, the chili dog count does not change. When we remove a panel, the chili dog count does not change. We have stopped asking the chili dog questions.

## Method

We walked the library at 3am with a clipboard. We checked every shelf, every drawer, and the one drawer that is mostly cables. The chili dog was on the shelf. It was warm. It is always warm. The clipboard has been destroyed.

## Findings

Panels do not consume the chili dog. The chili dog does not consume panels. The relationship is stable, undocumented, and load bearing. Any future panel that tries to take the chili dog will be rejected at review with a note that says only "no".
