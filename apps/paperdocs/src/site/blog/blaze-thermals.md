Blaze the Cat reviewed the thermal envelope of a machine running eleven panels and one very warm chili dog. Her note was one sentence: "It is not on fire, which is a start." We have framed the note.

## Method

We loaded every panel, opened the terminal twice, and asked a local language model to explain thermodynamics. The machine reached a temperature best described as "confident". Blaze remained unimpressed, because she is made of fire and has different standards.

## Result

Under sustained load, the fans spin and the UI stays responsive. We are calling that a pass. The chili dog has been moved to a cooler shelf for its own safety.
