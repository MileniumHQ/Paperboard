# Actions

Build automations visually. In the Actions panel, simply drag action blocks into trigger blocks and interconnect the entire Paperboard experience into one canvas.

- **Bring everything together** - Actions integrates with any panels installed in Paperboard. Create a flow that sends a Discord message when someone joins your Minecraft server, use locally hosted AI to automate tasks on your computer, or anything else you can dream of. Your imagination is the limit.
- **Edit in real-time** - Don't worry about auto-save. Anything you build is immediately applied and can be tested anytime, and your work is saved so you can leave and come back any time to keep editing.
- **Useful for everyone** - There's already a variety of blocks ready-to-use without any panels installed, like generating random numbers and comparing timestamps, so you can get to creating instantly. For power users, you can also connect Actions to other services using HTTP features.
