# Drive

A simple place for your files, on the computer Paperboard runs on.

- **Drop files in** - Drag files onto Drive, or press Upload. Big files are fine.
- **See what's there** - Photos, films, PDFs, documents and more show as pictures, and open in a preview.
- **Keep it tidy** - Make folders, rename, and drag files into folders.
- **Automate it** - Flows in the Blocks panel can list, read, write, move and delete files.
