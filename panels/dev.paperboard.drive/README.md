# Drive

All your files in one place, on the computer Paperboard runs on, and open from anywhere.

- **Drop files in** - Drag files onto Drive, or press Upload. Big files are fine.
- **Plug in a drive** - USB drives and SD cards show up in the sidebar as soon as they're plugged in. Eject them from Drive when you're done.
- **Link your folders** - Bring folders like Pictures or Documents into Drive. Their files stay right where they are.
- **See what's there** - Photos, films, PDFs, documents and more show as pictures, and open in a preview.
- **Keep it tidy** - Make folders, rename, and drag files into folders.
- **Automate it** - Flows in the Blocks panel can list, read, write, move and delete files in Drive's own folder.

Nothing is copied. Drive's own files live in its folder in the Paperboard data folder (`files/dev.paperboard.drive/files`). A plugged-in drive's files stay on the drive, and a linked folder's files stay in the folder. Which folders are linked is kept in `files/dev.paperboard.drive/places.json`.
