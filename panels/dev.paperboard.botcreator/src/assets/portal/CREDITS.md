# Portal screenshots

The four images in this folder are screenshots of the Discord Developer Portal
from the documentation of **discord.py** (<https://github.com/Rapptz/discord.py>,
`docs/images/`). They are cropped, and `4-privileged-intents.webp` joins two
parts of one screenshot. The portal's design has changed since they were taken.
