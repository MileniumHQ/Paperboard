Chat with language models that run on your own hardware. AI talks to Ollama on your computer, so your conversations stay there.

- **Local models.** Download models from the Ollama library and switch between them per chat.
- **Web search.** Let a model look things up with DuckDuckGo when a question needs current information.
- **You stay in control.** Tools ask for your permission before they run.
- **History.** Every chat is saved on this computer and can be picked up later.
