Run a dedicated Minecraft server from your dashboard. Game Server downloads the server software, keeps it running, and gives you the controls you would otherwise reach for a terminal to use.

- **Any flavor.** Vanilla, Paper and Fabric servers, installed and updated for you.
- **World map.** See your world rendered from its region files.
- **Players.** Watch who is online and manage operators, bans and the whitelist.
- **Plugins and mods.** Search Modrinth and install plugins or mods in a click.
