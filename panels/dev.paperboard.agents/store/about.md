Text an agent the way you'd text a friend. The Agent panel gives it a computer of its own, so instead of telling you how to do something, it goes and does it.

- **Your choice of brain** - Run it on Codex, Claude or OpenCode, signed in with your own account, and pick the model it thinks with. OpenCode has free models too.
- **A computer of its own** - It browses the web in a browser of its own, and you can watch it work or take over at any time. It never sees your files or your network.
- **Apps made for you** - Ask for a planner, a tracker or a little game, and it builds an app you can open right beside the chat.
- **Remembers what matters** - It keeps short facts and longer notes as you talk. Read through everything it remembers, and make it forget anything with a click.
- **Routines** - Ask it to do something later or regularly, like "text me a flag fact every weekday at 9", and stop a routine whenever you like.
- **Works with your panels** - Let it start a game server or post a Discord message through your other panels, or use it in the Actions panel to have your automations text it and ask it questions.
- **More tools** - Connect MCP servers to give it new abilities, with their keys kept safe in Paperboard's vault.
