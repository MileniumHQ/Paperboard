Text an agent like you'd text a friend. It has a computer of its own, so instead of telling you how to do something, it does it.

- **Your choice of brain** - Use ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or another provider, including free ones through OpenCode.
- **A computer of its own** - It browses the web in its own browser. Watch it work, or take over any time.
- **Your computer, if you allow it** - It asks before it uses a folder or runs a command, and you can take anything back on the Access page.
- **Apps made for you** - Ask for a planner, a tracker or a little game, and open it right beside the chat.
- **Files in the chat** - It can send you pictures, audio and video.
- **Memories and routines** - It remembers what matters and does things on a schedule. Make it forget anything in a click.
- **Works with your panels** - Have it start a game server or post to Discord, or text it from the Blocks panel.
- **More tools** - Connect MCP servers to give it new abilities, with their keys kept in Paperboard's vault.
