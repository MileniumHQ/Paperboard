Chat with AI models locally without sacrifice. Rather than relying on models in data centers, the Paperboard AI panel lets you easily pick and install open-source models to run on your machine, without sending any of your data to the cloud.

- **No setup required** - Simply install the panel and pick a model to use. The panel downloads and initiates setup of the model for you, without changing any settings on your computer.
- **Upload files** - On supported models, read & analyze images, text, and code all by simply dragging and dropping the files. With local models, nothing is sent to the cloud.
- **Coding agents** - Bring Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and many more into the panel. Pick a project, choose a mode, and approve what the agent does before it runs.
- **More connections** - Prefer a model hosted somewhere else? Connect any OpenAI-compatible endpoint and keep your favorite models from every connection in one list.
- **Quick ask** - Ask your model a question straight from your Home tab without opening the panel.
